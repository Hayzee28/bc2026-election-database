About this database

This site reorganizes the Elections BC 2026 General Local Elections candidate register into a searchable public database. Candidate names, jurisdictions, offices, affiliations, financial-agent names and source-page references are preserved from the source dataset.

Service-address note

Financial-agent service addresses are not reproduced here. They can be viewed in the official Elections BC PDF linked above.
